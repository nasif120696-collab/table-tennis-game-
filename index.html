Table tennis

Send the ball past your opponent's paddle. First to 11 points wins a game, by at least 2.

Move
W / S move up and down, A / D step back and forward (arrow keys work too). Mouse or touch moves the bat anywhere on your side.
2 players: Player 1 uses W A S D, Player 2 uses the arrow keys (left steps forward).
Serve
Press Space or tap. The ball is tossed, then struck. The serve changes every 2 points.
Angle
Hit with the edge of the paddle to steer the ball. Every hit makes it faster.
Smash
Press Space (Enter for Player 2), click or tap as the ball comes to you (an orange ring shows a good ball). The bat glows and your next hit is a fast topspin smash. It uses a third of your Smash meter. Smash high balls; low ones often go into the net.
Spin
Move the bat sideways as you hit to curve the ball. Hold Shift for a soft backspin push (or a short serve). Hold Q (M for Player 2) to lob the ball high, which gives a smash chance to the other side. To aim a serve, hold W or S during the toss.
Rules
The ball must bounce once on your side before you hit it, and must clear the net and land on the table. A ball that clips the net cord and lands in is played on; on a serve it is a let.
Crowd
Spectators watch the match and applaud good points. Trophy ceremonies follow cups, leagues and matches. Turn either off in the menu.

Press P.
