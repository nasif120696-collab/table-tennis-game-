Table tennis

Send the ball past your opponent's paddle. First to 11 points wins, by at least 2.

Move
1 player: W / S, arrow keys, mouse or touch.
2 players: Player 1 uses W / S, Player 2 uses ↑ / ↓. On touch, drag on your half.
Serve
Press Space or tap the table. The serve changes every 2 points.
Angle
Hit with the edge of the paddle to steer the ball. Every hit makes it faster.
Pause
Press P.
